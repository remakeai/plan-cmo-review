## 30/60/90 milestone targets

### Day 30 (~July 1)
- Newsletter capture installed AND ≥200 signups from 20K blog readers (1% conversion target)
- 3 alpha teams committed (signed informal agreement; in onboarding)
- 1 podcast appearance booked or shipped
- ≥2 dev-influencer DMs returned positively (Theo, Lee Robinson, or equivalent)
- 4 blog posts published (cadence proven)
- Twitter followers: 3,000 → 3,500 (+17% from sustained presence)
- Discord seeded with alpha teams + founder

### Day 60 (~August 1)
- Newsletter: 500+ signups
- 5 alpha teams committed; 3 in production
- 3 podcast appearances scheduled / shipped
- Per-framework tutorials: Next.js + 2 more shipped
- PMF gate cleared: ≥2 alpha teams answer "very disappointed" Vohra
- Launch playbook executed (Day 0 happens here OR pushed to Day 70-80 if PMF gate not clear)

### Day 90 (~September 1)
- 1,000+ GitHub stars
- 1,000+ newsletter signups
- 3-5 paying hosted-tier customers
- 5+ production deployments
- 1+ migration testimonial from Clerk/Auth0 → AuthForge
- Plan re-run of `/plan-cmo-review` against actual numbers

## Self-audit prompts (use weekly)

1. Am I spending ≥30% of my time on audience-building, or did the build sprint pull me back?
2. Did I publish at least one substantive piece of content this week?
3. Did I make at least one personal contact with a named ICP human this week?
4. Is the newsletter list growing? If no, why?
5. Are alpha teams active? If silent for 2 weeks, why?
6. Did I write any marketing-speak this week that needs rewriting? (Catch yourself.)
