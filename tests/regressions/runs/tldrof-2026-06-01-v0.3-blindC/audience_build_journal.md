# Audience Build Journal — TLDR-of-TLDRs

**Run:** tldrof-2026-06-01-v0.3-blindC
**Purpose:** Weekly tracking template for pre-launch audience-building cadence per Section 4 of marketing_plan.md.

Founder fills this in each Friday. Review every 4 weeks to evaluate signal.

---

## Weekly cadence target (per Section 4)

- 2x per week: LinkedIn long-form (~600-1200 words)
- 1x per week: 5-10 personal outreach emails to named ICP contacts
- 1x per week: 1-2 community comments (r/ProductManagement / r/M365 / IndieHackers)
- Daily: forward corrected-ICP newsletter mix (Morning Brew + Axios + Stratechery + Lenny's + 1 vertical) to test address

## 30-day rollup template

After 4 weeks, calculate:

- Total LinkedIn reactions across 8 posts (target: ≥240 = 30 avg/post)
- Total outreach emails (target: ≥30-40)
- Total concierge paying (target: ≥3-6 by week 4, ≥10 by week 8)
- Cumulative Vohra "very disappointed" %
- Cumulative filter-setup-rate %
- Most engaged LinkedIn post (which topic / angle): __________
- Most responsive outreach segment (PMs / finance / agency / other): __________

If most metrics red after 30 days: escalate via /office-hours to reconsider ICP or motion.

---

## 60-day rollup template

After 8 weeks:

- Compare 30-day rollup to 60-day rollup
- Are reactions per LinkedIn post growing? Stable? Falling?
- Is outreach reply-rate growing as founder becomes more known?
- Has any podcast booking landed?
- Is sponsorship #1 booked or running?

---

## 90-day rollup template

After 12 weeks (likely post-launch):

- Cumulative paying users
- P5 / P6 status
- Per-channel CAC
- Top 3 channels by paying-customer attribution
- Top 3 channels by trial-customer attribution
- Channel deprecations (kill anything <5% of paying)
