Many founder marketing plans miss this and waste weeks pitching the newsletter.

## 3. Linear founder playbook (canonical-success comparable detail)

**Query:** `Karri Saarinen Linear founder story invite only beta launch playbook`

**Key findings:**
- Linear founded 2019 by Karri Saarinen + Jori Lallo + Tuomas Artman. Saarinen ex-Airbnb design systems.
- Private invite-only beta. Exited beta June 2020 (~12-18 months invite-only).
- Waitlist grew to 10,000+ via Saarinen's build-in-public Twitter strategy ("live, public changelog").
- Strategic positioning: term "issue tracking" was deliberate filter — engineers understand it, non-engineers don't seek it out.
- "Go slow to go fast" — ~3 years before hiring first marketer.
- Valuation reported in $400M+ range (Thoughtlytics piece).

**Sources:**
- https://www.thoughtlytics.com/newsletter/linear-playbook-growth-engine
- https://review.firstround.com/linears-path-to-product-market-fit/
- https://www.growth-letter.com/p/this-startup-had-10000-people-on
- https://sequoiacap.com/article/linear-spotlight/
- https://research.contrary.com/company/linear

**Plan impact:** Hard precedent for canonical-success comparable in 1b. Threadline's "Show HN on day N" plan is the opposite of Linear's actual playbook. Used to anchor PMF-gate question in Section 5a.

## 4. Superhuman PMF framework (Vohra 40% gate)

**Query:** `Superhuman Rahul Vohra 40 percent very disappointed product market fit`

**Key findings:**
- Vohra adapted Sean Ellis's "how would you feel if you could no longer use this product" / "very disappointed" benchmark.
- 40% benchmark = standard for product-market fit (Ellis's research across hundreds of startups).
- Superhuman started at 22%, segmented to the loving cohort, iterated to 33% then 47% (Q2-Q3 2017).

**Sources:**
- https://review.firstround.com/how-superhuman-built-an-engine-to-find-product-market-fit/
- https://businessofsoftware.org/talks/product-market-fit-engine/
- https://coda.io/@rahulvohra/superhuman-product-market-fit-engine
- https://www.churn.fm/episode/how-superhuman-avoids-churn-by-systematically-increasing-product-market-fit

**Plan impact:** Anchors Section 5a (PMF-signal gate, not calendar gate) with named, sourced precedent. Used to override the founder's "5 paying companies by day 30" calendar gate.

## 5. Jira pricing (verifying competitor band)

**Query:** `Jira Atlassian software pricing per user 2026`

**Key findings:**
- Free (≤10 users), Standard $7.91-8.15/user, Premium $14.54-16.00/user, Enterprise ~$20-25/user.
- Volume discount: 500-user team avg ~$6.82/user on Standard.
- "Hidden cost" reality: $7.91/user nominal can become $200-350/user/year with integrations and Premium features.

**Sources:**
- https://www.atlassian.com/software/jira/pricing
- https://costbench.com/software/project-management/jira/
- https://ones.com/blog/jira-pricing-explained-why-you-should-consider-other-options/
- https://tech.co/project-management-software/jira-pricing

**Plan impact:** Confirms Jira sits in $8-25/user band, similar to Linear. Threadline at $40 is at the top of the mid-market eng-tooling band overall (alongside Datadog, PagerDuty, GitHub Enterprise) — reinforces Section 6a finding that $40 implies mission-critical positioning.
