## Cadence rules (apply throughout)

- **Customer dev:** 2 interviews/week minimum.
- **Content:** start at 1 founder-bylined post/week, scale to 2 if signal positive (>100 LinkedIn reactions or >5 inbound DMs per post).
- **Community:** 30 min/day in Rands Slack as helpful presence. NOT posting; answering questions, contributing to threads.
- **Outreach:** 1 podcast pitch/week.
- **Conference floor:** every relevant LeadDev event in driving distance.

## Stop-the-line conditions

- **Week 4:** if <3 paid design partners signed, customer-dev process is failing. Stop content cadence; debug customer-dev process for 1 week before resuming.
- **Week 8:** if Vohra baseline <25%, product is not close to PMF. Stop launch prep; iterate product for 4 weeks before re-checking.
- **Week 12:** if Vohra still <40%, LAUNCH DATE MOVES. Do not ship publicly on calendar pressure. Linear precedent: ~12-18 months in private beta. Superhuman precedent: PMF score gates the next investment in growth.

## Anti-patterns to refuse (audience-build edition)

1. **"I'll just post more on Twitter."** — Vague; no cadence; no topic plan; refused.
2. **"I'll batch all content for week 5 in week 4."** — Batch-fail risk; content production interrupts customer-dev rhythm; refused.
3. **"I'll skip customer dev because I've already done it informally."** — Informal interviews don't generate case studies, don't generate Vohra data, don't generate design-partner relationships; refused.
4. **"I'll launch on the calendar even if Vohra is at 25%."** — See stop-the-line; refused.
5. **"Ex-Google credibility will carry the launch."** — It won't with this ICP; see ICP doc; refused as standalone distribution strategy.
