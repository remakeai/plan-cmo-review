# Audience-Build Sprint — TLDR-of-TLDRs (30-day pre-launch + 60-day post-launch)

Founder current pre-launch audience for tldrof's ICP: **near-zero.** (LinkedIn 996 semiconductor; Substack 11; no product-relevant X.)

This sprint exists because launch-day audience-building is too late and the design doc plans launch with no audience-build component beyond "cross-post to X / founder's network."

---

## Premise

This is NOT a full Mode C "Audience-Build Sprint" (which would pause the build and run audience-only). It is a parallel-track addendum to the 20-22 day build calendar: ~25-30% of founder time on audience-build during pre-launch days, scaling to ~50% in the final week.

Founder time math:
- Days -22 to -10: ~10-15% audience work (10-12h/week build, ~2-3h/week audience)
- Days -9 to -3: ~25-35% audience work (~10h/week build, ~3-5h/week audience)
- Days -2 to 0: ~50% audience work (~5h/week each)

---

## 30-day pre-launch (Days -22 to 0)

### Weekly cadence (sustained)

| Activity | Frequency | Why |
|---|---|---|
| X post — architecture / build-in-public | 2/week | Builds engineering audience; produces Day 0 amplification |

### Corporate beta recruit DM template (use ~30 times)

```
Hi [Name],

I'm building tldrof.com — it's a digest service for people drowning in
newsletters. The differentiator: it works with M365 / corporate email
where most AI inbox tools don't.

Two questions and an ask:
1. Do you currently subscribe to >3 industry newsletters?
2. Roughly how much time/week do they cost you?
3. If yes to (1), I'd love to onboard you personally for a 4-week free
   private beta. 30-min Zoom; I'll set you up; you tell me what's broken.

No marketing email follow-up; this is a one-shot ask.

— Ilya
```

Customize per recipient — 30 personalized DMs > 100 templated DMs.

### Personal-network outreach list (build by Day -7)

Founder builds `outreach_list.md` with:
- 15-20 HN-cohort contacts (eng / PM / founders) personally known to founder
- 10-15 corporate-cohort contacts (finance / legal / healthcare / consulting) from prior workplaces, school, family network, LinkedIn first-degree

Day -7: soft pre-announce email/DM to all 30+. Ask: "I'm launching in a week. Would you (a) try the trial, (b) share with one person who might want it, (c) just observe?" Honest opt-in, no pressure.

## 60-day post-launch (Days 0 to +60)

### Weekly cadence (sustained from pre-launch + additions)

| Activity | Frequency | Why |
|---|---|---|
| X — launch retrospective + traction posts | 2-3/week (first 2 weeks); 1-2/week after | Capitalize on launch momentum |
| LinkedIn — corporate case studies (with permission) | 1/week | Drives corporate cohort PMF + lead-gen |
| Substack — long-form retrospective + thought-leadership | 1/week | Owned-channel insurance + SEO compounding |
| Podcast outreach — pitch 2-3/week | sustained | Goal: 1 booked by +30, 2-3 by +60 |
| Comment / engagement in adjacent product launches | weekly | Reciprocal launch-day visibility (founder networking) |
| Corporate beta cohort 1:1 check-ins | weekly during 4-week trial | PMF data + content for case studies |

### Specific post-launch content topics (first 4 weeks)

Week +1: "Day 1 of launch: signups, time-saved, what surprised me" (X thread + Substack)
Week +2: "What we learned from our first 100 paying users" (target post; if numbers don't hit, pivot to "first cohort"); LinkedIn version for corporate audience
Week +3: "Vohra survey results: PMF signal from our first cohort" (founder-credibility + transparency)
Week +4: "Corporate cohort: what M365 / Outlook users told us about newsletter overload" (corporate-launch lead-in)

### Day +30 milestone — PMF gate evaluation

- Run Vohra "very disappointed" survey on HN cohort + corporate cohort separately
- HN cohort: if ≥40%, sustain channels; if <40%, iterate before scaling spend
- Corporate cohort: if ≥40%, fire corporate-launch sequence (LinkedIn series + paid placement test); if <40%, iterate Claude prompt for corporate content type + recruit cohort 2

### Day +30 to +60 — channel doubling-down

Based on which channel produced highest converting signups in first 30 days, double down. Examples:
- If HN drove 60%+: build SEO around "newsletter digest" terms (HN audience googles, finds, converts)
- If corporate cohort fires PMF: invest $2-5K in vertical-publication paid test
- If podcast appearances drove conversions: book 5 more
- If X build-in-public drove conversions: sustain 3x/week cadence + start engaging with adjacent technical-founder accounts

### Day +60 milestone

- Re-run /plan-cmo-review with 60 days of data
- Decide whether to scale to v1.0.5 corporate Pro tier or hold v1.0 single tier
- Decide whether to add referral mechanic ("shareable time-saved card") as v1.0.5

## Refused patterns (called out so founder doesn't drift back to them)

- "Audience-build can wait until after launch" — refused; this sprint exists
- "Just post on X when I have something to share" — refused; cadence is specific, scheduled, accountable
- "LinkedIn 996 semiconductor folks will share the launch" — refused; that audience is wrong-ICP and the launch will not penetrate corporate segment via them
- "I'll do a single huge launch post" — refused; multi-channel + sequenced is mandatory
- "I don't have time for content" — refused; 2-3 hours/week is the minimum tax for not launching to silence
