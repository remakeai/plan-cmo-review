# Launch Playbook — Threadline (sequenced, concierge-first)

Status: NOT a Show-HN-first sequence. Step 0.5 M0a flagged HN as wrong-audience-class for $40/seat B2B PM sold to EMs at 50-500-person SaaS. Launch is rebuilt around Linear's precedent (waitlist + hand-picked beta + bottom-up viral) compressed into 30/60/90 windows.

## Pre-launch window (Day -30 to Day -1)

### Day -30 to -25 (week 1)
- **Both founders:** build a list of 30-50 ex-Google contacts now at EM/TL/Director roles at 50-500-person SaaS companies. Source: LinkedIn 1st-degree connections + Google internal alumni groups + personal Rolodex.
- **Founder A:** draft a 4-sentence personal email template. Send 15-20 personalized variants over days -28 to -23. Goal: 8-10 design-partner calls booked.
- **Founder B:** create LinkedIn presence; post #1 build-in-public: "We're building a project tracker for engineering teams at 50-500-person SaaS — here's the specific workflow that breaks at this scale."
- **Both:** join Rands Leadership Slack and LeadDev Slack as themselves (not as "Threadline"). Begin reading + occasional substantive replies.

### Day -25 to -15 (weeks 2-3)
- 8-10 design-partner interviews completed; capture verbatim quotes
- Founder A: LinkedIn post #2 (specific learning from interviews)
- Founder B: LinkedIn post #3 (a design decision the team made and why)
- Synthesis post: "What 10 EMs at scale-up SaaS told us about why their PM stack is broken" — published on LinkedIn long-form + founders' personal blog
- Identify 5-10 design partners willing to be paying-beta cohort
- Begin concierge onboarding of first 3-5 cohort members: founder personally sets up workspace, imports data, joins first sprint meeting

### Day -15 to -7 (week 4 pre)
- Expand cohort to 5-10 paying companies via concierge onboarding
- Rands Slack: contribute 5-7 substantive replies; begin to be a recognizable handle
- Founder A: LinkedIn post #4 (deeper engineering blog post; "Building a Linear competitor: 3 things we got wrong in week 2")
- Light pre-announce email to founders' combined network (50 people from personal list + 30-50 ex-Google contacts already pitched): "we're launching publicly in 7 days; if you know an EM at a 50-500-person SaaS, would love a warm intro"

### Day -7 to -1
- Final landing page polish; ensure honest "Threadline vs Linear: better at X, worse at Y" section is live
- Press kit: founder photos, ex-Google credentials, cohort logos (with permission), product screenshots showing the real workflow
- Hold final concierge-cohort dependence check: ≥40% of cohort users answer "very disappointed" on the Vohra survey? If <40%, DELAY public launch — keep iterating with cohort. Calendar gate is conditional on PMF signal.

## Post-launch (Day +1 to Day +30)

### Day +1 to +3
- Respond to every comment on LinkedIn, HN, Rands posts personally. Within 1 hour during business hours.
- Capture: which channel drove the most ICP-aligned signups (track via UTM and signup-form question "how did you hear")
- Daily standup between founders: "what's working, what's not, what to double down on by tomorrow"

### Day +7
- Pitch 5 podcasts: Software Engineering Daily, Pragmatic Engineer podcast, Lenny's podcast, Engineering Enablement, LeadDev podcast. Pitch angle: "we just shipped a PM tool with a 30-day concierge-onboarded cohort of 10 EMs at scale-up SaaS; here's what we learned and what we got wrong."
- Pitch 1 LeadDev guest post (same angle)
- First case study draft: pick the strongest design-partner cohort company; ask permission; draft 1500-word write-up

### Day +14
- Long-form engineering blog post #2: "30 days in: what we learned, what we're killing, what's next"
- Begin SEO content infrastructure: ship 3-4 comparison-page drafts ("Linear vs Threadline for X", "Migrating from Jira: a guide")
- First product update post on LinkedIn

### Day +21
- Mid-month assessment: are we tracking toward 5-10 paying companies by day 30? If <50%, revisit Section 6 packaging (move to Option 2 free tier?) and Section 4 channel mix.

### Day +30
- Public retrospective post on LinkedIn and personal blog: "30 days. Real numbers. What worked, what didn't."
- Calendar-gate check: 5 paying companies achieved?
- Vohra "very disappointed" survey to all signups: ≥40% threshold?
- IF both gates passed → broaden launch to Product Hunt, podcast tour, paid content amplification
- IF either fails → DO NOT broaden. Concierge another cohort of 5-10 from new warm intros. Reassess at day 60.

## Tonal register reminders (per skill anti-pattern #13)

Words REFUSED in all launch copy:
- revolutionary, best-in-class, enterprise-grade, world-class, game-changing, seamless, robust, reimagined, next-gen, AI-powered (unless specifically demonstrating an AI feature with named tradeoff)

Words RECOMMENDED:
- specific workflow names, "we built X because of Y problem", "Linear is better at A; Threadline is better at B", named cohort companies, ex-Google credential disclosure, link to public roadmap, link to source-available code (if applicable)

## Risk callouts (per Section 5)
- HN-miss has near-zero downside (HN is awareness pulse, not primary)
- LinkedIn-algorithm risk is mitigated by parallel Rands + email channels
- Concierge bottleneck caps cohort at 10-15; design product for self-serve OR cap intentionally
