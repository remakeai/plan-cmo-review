# Audience-Build Journal — Threadline

Template for tracking pre-launch and early-launch cadence. Update weekly. Designed for honesty, not for highlight-reels.

## Baseline (Day -30)
- Total LinkedIn followers (Founder A): ___
- Total LinkedIn followers (Founder B): ___
- LinkedIn 1st-degree connections classified as ICP-aligned (EM/TL/Director at 50-500-person SaaS): ___ out of ___
- Email list size: ___
- Email list ICP-aligned: ___ (manual classification)
- X followers (combined): 200
- X followers ICP-aligned: ___
- Rands Leadership Slack: joined Y/N
- LeadDev Slack: joined Y/N
- Pragmatic Engineer subscriber: Y/N

## Weekly cadence targets

### Per founder per week
- LinkedIn posts: 2
- Rands Slack substantive replies (NOT promo): 3-5
- LeadDev Slack substantive replies: 1-2
- Design-partner emails: 5-10 in weeks 1-2, then 2-3/week after
- Design-partner calls: 3-5 in weeks 1-4, then 1-2/week after
- Long-form engineering blog post: 1 every 2 weeks starting week 3

### Combined weekly outputs
- LinkedIn posts: 4
- Slack contributions: 8-12 substantive
- Personal warm-intro emails sent: 10-20
- Design-partner calls completed: 6-10 in weeks 1-4

## Tracking rules

1. NEVER use a vanity metric without a downstream-conversion metric next to it. Likes is fine if paired with "qualified signups attributable to that post."
2. UTM-tag every link. No exceptions.
3. Weekly review: every Friday EOD. Both founders. 30 min.
4. If the same channel underperforms for 3 weeks running with no improvement, CUT it. Don't double down on dead channels.
5. If a channel surprises positively, immediately re-invest the next week's hours into it.

## Anti-patterns to refuse in this journal

- Bulk-sending the same message to N contacts and counting it as N personal outreach
- Counting Slack channel JOINS as community engagement
- Counting LinkedIn impressions instead of qualified inbound
- Skipping a week's update because "we were heads-down on product"
- Reframing missed targets as "we're learning"
