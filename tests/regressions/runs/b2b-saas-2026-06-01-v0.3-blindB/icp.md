# ICP — Threadline

## Named persona: "Priya, Director of Engineering at Series B SaaS"

### Demographics
- Director of Engineering at a 220-person Series B SaaS company (verticals: fintech infra, devops, AI tooling, vertical SaaS like legal/HR/healthcare ops)
- 2 years in current role, 10-14 years total experience
- Was a Senior EM → moved up to Director when company crossed 100
- Reports to a CTO who has 2-3 Directors under them
- Holds procurement authority up to ~$2K/mo on new tools without CFO sign-off
- Located in: US tech hubs (SF, NYC, Boston, Austin, Seattle), increasingly hybrid; secondary: London, Berlin, Toronto

### Team shape
- 4 engineering squads of 5-7 each reporting up to her (~22-30 people total)
- Mix of staff/senior/mid/junior with a Tech Lead per squad
- Stack: GitHub, Slack, Notion, Jira (inherited, hated), Datadog, PagerDuty, Linear (used by design team only)
- Async-first or hybrid-async; 1-2 standups per week per squad, not daily

### Current pain (the trigger that brings her to Threadline)
- Sprint planning takes 90 min/squad/sprint — 6+ hours of EM time per sprint cycle
- Cross-squad roadmap visibility is fractured between Jira epics, Notion docs, and Slack threads
- PR-to-ticket linking is a manual chore; status reports to CTO are 3+ hours of synthesis
- Q3 retro produced the third "Jira is killing our velocity" data point in 9 months
- Has had 2 internal threads about moving to Linear, blocked by procurement (existing Jira contract, multi-team dependencies)

### What she currently pays for in adjacent categories
- Pragmatic Engineer subscription ($150/yr) — personal expense
- Lenny's Newsletter Pro ($150/yr) — personal
- 1-2 industry podcasts ad-free (Software Engineering Daily, etc.) — personal
- Notion personal plan
- Company pays for: Jira ($7.75-15.25/seat × 30 = ~$300-450/mo), Datadog (~$15-30/host), PagerDuty (~$15-50/user)

### What she complains about in adjacent categories
- Jira: "configuration sprawl", "everything is 5 clicks deep", "the boards are slow", "search is broken"
- Notion: "great for docs, terrible for tracking work, can't be the project tool"
- Slack: "every project conversation gets lost in a thread"
- Existing PM tools: "they're all designed for software teams in general, not engineering teams specifically"

### Specific attention places (where Threadline must show up)
1. **Pragmatic Engineer** newsletter (Gergely Orosz) — guest-post or sponsored slot is high-leverage
2. **Lenny's Newsletter** — guest piece on engineering productivity tools
3. **Rands Leadership Slack** — 30K EMs, recommendation-driven discovery; founder must contribute genuinely for 2+ weeks before any mention
4. **LeadDev Slack** (~7K members) and LeadDev conferences/newsletter
5. **Software Engineering Daily** / **Engineering Enablement** podcasts — interview format
6. **staffeng.com** / Will Larson's writing
7. **r/ExperiencedDevs** — lurks rather than posts, but reads top threads
8. **LinkedIn** — follows specific voices: Karri Saarinen, Charity Majors, Will Larson, Lara Hogan, Camille Fournier, Pat Kua, Gergely Orosz

### Specific objection patterns
- "Why would I switch to a tool I've never heard of when Linear exists at $8?" (handles via: clear wedge + named peer companies using it)
- "Our procurement cycle is 60 days; your 14-day trial expires before we can buy" (handles via: extended trial OR free tier OR concierge enterprise-style approach)
- "Linear has 5 years of polish and integrations; you have 30 days of code" (handles via: founder-track-record OR ex-Google credentials OR specific wedge they care about MORE than polish)
- "$40/seat × 30 seats = $14K/yr. Linear is $2.9K/yr. What's the 5x value?" (handles via: explicit ROI math OR drop the price)
- "I've never heard of you; what happens to my team if you fold?" (handles via: data export guarantee, source-available code, named investors)

### Specific reasons she would convert
- A trusted EM peer in Rands Slack recommends it after using it for 2+ sprints
- A specific workflow she has been complaining about (e.g., multi-squad roadmap) is demonstrably 10x better in Threadline
- A demo or trial that completes her real-world sprint cycle end-to-end in <30 min setup
- Founder credibility: ex-Google + named investors + a CTO peer she trusts has tried it
- Migration path from Jira that doesn't require manual re-creation of 3K tickets

### What she will NOT respond to
- "Revolutionary" / "best-in-class" copy
- Founder vibes-only ("we built it because we hated Jira") without specific workflow proof
- Show HN promotional tone in any communication
- Aggressive upsell during trial
- AI-buzzword copy without specific demo
