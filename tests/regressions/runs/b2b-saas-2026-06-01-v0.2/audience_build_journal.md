## Decision triggers

- **Week 4:** If Pragmatic Engineer pitch has no response, escalate (warm intro via ex-Google peer who knows Gergely Orosz's team; offer guest essay vs sponsorship).
- **Week 6:** If <3 design partners onboarded, the warm-intro pool is thinner than assumed. Pivot — add cold outreach + Pragmatic Engineer sponsorship earlier.
- **Week 8:** Run first Vohra "very disappointed" survey. If <30%, the product is not ready for public launch. Extend sprint.
- **Week 10:** Procurement-readiness gate: SOC 2 Type I report? SSO live? If no, extend sprint and do NOT launch.
- **Week 12:** All launch pre-conditions met (Vohra ≥40%, 3 references, SOC 2 Type I, SSO live, audience runway). Proceed to launch playbook (Day -7 starts here).

## Weekly retrospective questions (Founder A and B together, 30 min Friday)

1. What's the strongest signal we got this week? (named person, named quote, named conversion)
2. What's the weakest assumption we noticed? (something we believed Monday and don't believe Friday)
3. What did we ship in product that's NOT validated by a real EM/TL conversation? (anti-feature watch)
4. What's the highest-leverage next-week action? (commit, single action, owner)
5. Are we on track for the Week 12 launch gate? (yes / behind / ahead — and why)
