## Anti-vanity rules

- Follower count is INPUT to audience-build, not the goal. Track named EM follows (count: how many followers are EMs at 50-500p companies you can name?). 50 named EM-follows > 500 random followers.
- Likes / comments are signal, NOT scoreboard. Conversations booked >> reactions.
- HN upvotes are NOT a metric for this build. If a Show HN happens organically pre-launch, do not chase it.
