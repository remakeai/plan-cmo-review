# Threadline — ICP specification

## Primary persona: Priya — Senior EM

**Status:** Synthesis / hypothesis until validated against ≥5 real EM interviews.

### Identity
- Senior Engineering Manager, 6-12 years out of college, 3-5 years in EM role
- 30-person backend platform team across 4 squads
- Reports to a VP Eng who reports to CTO
- Company: Series C fintech, 200-300p total headcount, $50-200M ARR range
- Tenure at current company: 18-30 months

### Tooling baseline (current state)
- Org migrated from Jira to Linear in Q4 2025 (driven by IC engineers + a TL initiative)
- Slack for sync, Notion for docs, GitHub for code, PagerDuty for incidents, Datadog for obs
- Pays for Pragmatic Engineer paid sub ($150/yr) personally; team subscription to Linear $480/mo (30 seats x $16)

### Where her attention lives RIGHT NOW
1. **Rands Leadership Slack** (lurker, occasional poster in #management and #engineering-leadership)
2. **Pragmatic Engineer newsletter** (reads weekly, paid sub)
3. **Lenny's Newsletter + Podcast** (free sub; listens to podcast on commute)
4. **LeadDev annual conference** (attended last 2 years)
5. **Software Engineering Daily podcast** (occasional episodes)
6. **LinkedIn** (browses 2-3x/week; follows 50-100 EM/CTO accounts)
7. **HN comments occasionally**, rarely the front page

### Adjacent-category willingness-to-pay evidence
- Pragmatic Engineer: $150/yr personally
- Linear: $480/mo team
- Notion: ~$200/mo team
- Slack: budget-line, ~$1500/mo team
- Datadog: $$$$ enterprise budget
- Implication: a $25-40/seat tool in the eng-leadership category is well within band IF the value is clear

### Active pain candidates (TBD — confirm via interviews)
1. **Cross-team dependency tracking** — Linear's per-team focus breaks down when 4 squads have interlocking work
2. **Sprint-level analytics for VP Eng reporting** — Linear has metrics but they aren't framed for "what did we ship vs commit"
3. **Roadmap-vs-execution gap** — quarterly planning lives in Notion, execution lives in Linear, gap is manual
4. **Headcount-aware capacity planning** — when 2 people are on PTO, replanning is manual

Founders must pick ONE wedge from interviews. "We do all 4" is the failure mode.

### Objections she would raise
- **Price:** "We just standardized on Linear; you're 2.5x. Justify."
- **Trust:** "Who are you? Two ex-Googlers I've never heard of."
- **Security:** "Do you have SOC 2? My InfoSec team will ask. We can't even start a pilot without it."
- **Switching cost:** "We just migrated from Jira to Linear. I'm not doing another migration in 6 months."
- **Survival:** "Will you exist in 2 years? I don't want to be on Mortuary 2026."

### Reasons she would convert
- A named specific pain Linear can't fix AND her team is hitting that pain
- A peer-EM recommendation (Rands Slack, LeadDev, ex-coworker DM)
- Concierge-onboard offer signaling "we'll make this work for you specifically"
- Founder credibility — ex-Google helps but only as table-stakes; what matters is named teams shipped + visible thinking via LinkedIn / Pragmatic Engineer
- Add-on positioning ("works WITH your Linear") if founders pivot to integration play

### Specific outreach hooks (founders' use)
- "I led X at Google; we built Y because Linear couldn't do Z. Would you spend 30 min telling me if Z is a real pain for your team?"
- LinkedIn DM after she comments on an EM-thought-leadership post; offer 30 min of "I'd love to learn from your tooling stack — and I'll share what we're building if it's interesting."

## Anti-personas (NOT the ICP)

- **IC engineers at any company size** — users, not buyers. Will love Threadline, won't pay. Useful for bottom-up funnel only.
- **Solo founders / indie hackers** — wrong scale, wrong budget, wrong pain.
- **CTOs at <50p companies** — already on Linear free tier or GitHub Projects; no budget for additional tooling.
- **VPs Eng at >500p companies** — enterprise procurement; sales cycle 6-12 months; out of scope for v1.
- **PMs (not EMs)** — different buying committee; Threadline is EM-focused.
- **Ex-Googler friends at unicorns** — feel like ICP but actually wrong (Google-scale infra muscle memory makes them a misleading dogfood subject; see M0e).
