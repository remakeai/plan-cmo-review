# Threadline — Launch Playbook (sequenced, multi-channel)

**Gate condition:** do NOT launch publicly until PMF-signal gate fires (≥40% "very disappointed" OR ≥3 unprompted EM-to-EM referrals OR ≥3 teams with bottom-up IC-engineer pull). Calendar launch is contraindicated for retention-driven seat businesses.

## Day 30-90

- Product Hunt as a SEPARATE launch event around day 60-75 (different audience; needs hunter relationship by then)
- LeadDev conference proposal for next event
- Second cornerstone content asset published
- Begin paid experimentation IF organic channels show CAC < $500/seat

## Risk callouts (multi-channel resilience)

- **HN miss?** LinkedIn is the anchor. Lose HN, still have the LI launch.
- **LI algorithm suppression?** Cold outreach + design-partner referrals still produce inbound.
- **Cohort doesn't convert?** Halt launch; do not blow audience capital. Re-validate wedge.
- **Linear shipping the same feature in their next release?** Wedge selection MUST favor depth-of-build (a feature that takes 6+ months to replicate), not "the obvious thing Linear missed."

## Channel coverage check (forced ≥3 channels)

✓ LinkedIn (PRIMARY)
✓ Cold outreach to ex-Google EM list (PRIMARY)
✓ Show HN (TACTICAL entry point, not primary)
✓ Pragmatic Engineer / Lenny's earned coverage (EARN, slow)
✓ Rands Leadership Slack (delayed, requires rep)

5 channels named with sequence. Single-channel launch refused.
