## Failure modes to watch for in the journal

If you find yourself writing any of these in the "what didn't work" section repeatedly, treat it as a structural problem:

1. **"Didn't have time for Twitter this week"** — the time-allocation commitment is failing; this is the #1 cause of audience-build sprint failure
2. **"Engagement is low / followers aren't growing"** in weeks 1-4 — normal; compounding happens around week 6-12; do NOT pivot in the first 4 weeks
3. **"Engagement is low" in weeks 8+** — content quality or positioning problem; iterate
4. **"Conversions are low from Show HN traffic"** — expected (HN is high-curiosity, low-intent); conversion should be higher from concierge-onboarded users
5. **"Readless keeps coming up in HN comments"** — expected; have the response template ready
6. **"Founder feels burnt out"** — sustainable pace is ~20-30 hr/week on audience-build; cut elsewhere (product, polish, fancy features) before cutting audience-build
