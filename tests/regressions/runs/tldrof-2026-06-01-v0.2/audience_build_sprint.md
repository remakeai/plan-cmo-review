# Audience-Build Sprint — tldrof.com

**Mode C output.** Primary deliverable for a founder with zero ICP-relevant audience.

**Situation:** ex-Micron engineer, first consumer product, ~996 LinkedIn followers (wrong ICP — hardware engineers), ~11 Substack subscribers, zero Twitter/X presence, mid-build (Day 5 of 20-22 day window). Domain registered. No marketing surface for the product yet.

**Brutal honest assessment:** the founder is 60-90 days BEHIND where the canonical-success comparable playbooks (Superhuman, Readwise, Levels-pattern) say the audience-build should already be. The choice now is between:
- **Option A:** delay launch 60-90 days to build audience first (skill orthodoxy)
- **Option B:** ship per the calendar bound (day 20-22) as planned, BUT shift the founder's time-allocation heavily toward audience-build starting NOW and treat the launch as continuation of beta, not victory lap

**Recommendation:** Option B with discipline. The build is too far along to delay 60 days; the audience cannot be built to canonical level in 16 days. Ship the calendar launch as a discovery event, then run the 90-day audience-build sprint AROUND it (with the launch in the middle of the sprint, not at the end).

This file is the 30/60/90-day audience-build sprint.

---

## Foundational commitments

Before any of the cadence below makes sense, lock these:

1. **20% minimum of remaining build-window time on audience-build, starting now.** ~2.5 hr/day if working 12 hr/day. Most days will go over.
2. **50%+ post-launch time on audience-build for 60 days.** The product is "good enough"; the audience is not.
3. **One PRIMARY compounding channel:** **Twitter/X build-in-public.** Per Levels-pattern: 10-year horizon, but the first 60 days establish the cadence and identity.
4. **Two SECONDARY channels:** Substack long-form (cross-post Twitter threads as essays, plus 1 weekly original) + cold email outreach (concierge-onboarding pipeline).
5. **One INFRASTRUCTURE channel:** the existing 996-follower LinkedIn base. Post 1 long-form/week minimum. Don't expect ICP conversion but the founder-journey content compounds for 1-2 referrals AND repositions the founder's LinkedIn brand toward consumer SaaS for future opportunities.
6. **CUT EVERYTHING ELSE.** No SEO content in the 90 days (Readless owns it; revisit at day 90+). No Reddit beyond r/SideProject day +2. No paid ads. No podcasts (until you have a launch story to tell). No Product Hunt before day +7. No Indie Hackers presence before day +2.

## What this sprint refuses to do

- **No SEO content for the first 30 days.** Readless owns the SERP turf; entering pre-launch is wasted effort. Start at day +30 when there's a tldrof launch story to seed comparison posts with.
- **No paid acquisition for the first 60 days.** No data to optimize against; founder time on organic compounds; paid is for v1.0.5+.
- **No Product Hunt before day +7.** Different audience, deserves its own moment, don't burn it on day 0.
- **No podcast guesting before day +30.** Need a launch story to tell.
- **No vague "I'll post more on Twitter" commitments.** Cadence is locked above. Daily. Measure.
- **No "I'll do audience-build after launch."** That order is wrong. Audience starts NOW; launch is in the middle.

## Honest expected outcomes

Realistic 90-day audience numbers given zero starting point:

| Metric | Day 0 | Day 22 (launch) | Day 52 (+30) | Day 82 (+60) | Day 112 (+90) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Twitter followers | 0 | 100-300 | 400-700 | 700-1.2K | 1K-1.8K |
| Substack subscribers | 11 | 30-60 | 80-150 | 150-300 | 300-500 |
| LinkedIn followers (relevant) | ~0 | +10-30 | +30-80 | +80-200 | +200-400 |
| Paying customers | 0 | 0-5 (private beta) | 15-25 (P6 / P5) | 25-60 | 50-150 |
| MRR | $0 | $0-45 | $135-225 | $225-540 | $450-1350 |

**These numbers are based on solo-founder consumer-SaaS audience-build benchmarks at zero starting point.** Pieter Levels' 10-year timeline gave him 600K followers and $132K MRR by month 18 on Photo AI; tldrof in 90 days is shooting for ~1% of those follower numbers and ~1% of that MRR, which is realistic for a solo-founder consumer subscription with no prior audience.

**If actual numbers are 50% of the above:** the plan is still alive but compounding more slowly. Stay the course; recalibrate at day 90.

**If actual numbers are <25% of the above:** there's a positioning or product problem. Time for the next `/plan-cmo-review` to surface it.

---

## When to bail on this plan

Re-evaluate the audience-build approach if at day +60 ALL of:
- Twitter followers <300
- Substack subscribers <50
- Paying customers <10
- Engagement rate on posts <1%

That combination signals the channel mix is wrong, the messaging is wrong, or the product-market fit is weaker than P5/P6 implied. Re-run `/plan-cmo-review` from scratch — possibly with `/office-hours` first to rethink positioning.

If the founder is hitting >50% of the expected outcomes at day +30, the plan is working. Stay the course.
