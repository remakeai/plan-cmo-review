## 30/60/90 post-launch extension

### Day 1-30 post-launch (2026-06-19 → 2026-07-18)

- LinkedIn: 1-2 posts/wk maintaining build-in-public + sharing first-month traction
- SEO content: 2 new articles (Sarah-vertical + Alex-comparison ratio)
- Community: continue contributing in 3 communities; do NOT spam tldrof links
- Podcast outreach: 5 pitches to legal-tech / finance-tech / Alex-side podcasts (4-12 week lead times)
- Cold outreach: 10 more Sarah-shape contacts as warm intros from concierge-cohort referrals
- Bensbites/AlphaSignal sponsorship #1 placement if approved
- Refind paid placement test (~$200) to measure broad newsletter-reader CAC

### Day 31-60 post-launch

- Scale whichever channel produced trial signups in days 1-30. Specifically:
  - If paid sponsorship CAC <$30, run a second placement in a different vertical newsletter
  - If SEO articles starting to rank for any of 5 priority keywords, ship 4 more articles (target 10 total live by day 60)
  - If podcast pitches landed, do 1-2 recordings; bank for day 90+ release
- Cold outreach: 10 more contacts per week
- Begin newsletter-publisher partnership conversations (Meco-style $4-5/new-paying-customer affiliate)

### Day 61-90 post-launch

- Integrate any partnerships landed. Cross-promote with one or more newsletter publishers.
- Ship referral mechanic if v1.0.5 includes it (two-sided $5-off-each)
- Founder appears on 1-2 podcasts that were pitched in pre-launch / day 1-30
- Retrospective post + Substack write-up of 90 days (becomes SEO-compounding asset itself)
