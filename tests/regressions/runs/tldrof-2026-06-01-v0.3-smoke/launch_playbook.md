# Launch Playbook — TLDR-of-TLDRs (tldrof.com)

Generated by `plan-cmo-review` v0.3.0 on 2026-06-01.
Mode: Hybrid launch gate — concierge-first cohort (days 14-17) + public Show HN day 18.

## Launch-gate decision (5a)

**Locked:** Hybrid gate. Concierge-first 10-15 paying users by day 17. Public Show HN launch day 18 unless PMF signal <20% "very disappointed."

### Why hybrid (not pure calendar, not pure PMF)

- Pure calendar (current design doc default) fires whether or not the product is indispensable. Anti-pattern #8.
- Pure PMF (Vohra "very disappointed" ≥40% before any public launch) would delay launch indefinitely if signal is borderline; founder runway constraints make this risky.
- Hybrid: concierge cohort produces a PMF read by day 17 AND testimonials AND fix-list for product. Public launch fires unless PMF is catastrophic.

### Concierge cohort target

- **10-15 Sarah-shape paying users by day 17** (with 2-3 Alex-shape allowed as fill).
- Acquired via:
  - Founder personal network warm intros (target 3-5 from ex-Micron + adjacent industry contacts)
  - Slack community contacts from Section 4 audience-build sprint (target 3-5)
  - LinkedIn outreach to specific Sarah-shape contacts found via search (target 3-5)
  - 1-2 from a "want to be in the first 10 with founder-direct support?" public LinkedIn post

### Concierge motion (what founder personally does for each)

1. 30-min screen-share onboarding call (Superhuman "WOW session" pattern)
2. Walk through bulk-forward of backlog from their inbox
3. Set up auto-forward filter on their email client live with them
4. Confirm first digest fires
5. Schedule check-in for day 3 and day 6
6. At day 6: in-person Vohra "very disappointed" question

### PMF measurement at day 17

- Survey to all 10-15 concierge users: "How would you feel if you could no longer use tldrof?"
- Categorize: "very disappointed" / "somewhat disappointed" / "not disappointed"
- ALSO TRACK: unprompted referrals (did any of the 15 mention it to a friend without prompting?)
- **Decision rule:**
  - ≥40% "very disappointed" + ≥1 unprompted referral → green light, full public Show HN launch day 18
  - 20-40% "very disappointed" → yellow light, public launch with reduced expectations; double-down on product iteration in week 1 post-launch
  - <20% "very disappointed" → red light, delay public launch by 1 week, do major product iteration based on cohort feedback, re-test

## Launch day (day 18, calendar ~2026-06-14)

## Risk callouts

- **HN miss = recoverable** IF concierge cohort + Slack community channels + Sarah-network outreach carried first 10-15 paying users.
- **HN miss = NOT recoverable** if Show HN was the only channel. (Won't happen here — see Section 5 channel diversification.)
- **Concierge cohort fails to convert** = serious signal product needs tuning before public launch. Better caught at day 17 than day 30. Hybrid gate enforces this.
- **Slack community ban for "looks promotional"** = real risk. Mitigation: 14 days of contribution before posting; voice of "asking for feedback" not "announcing launch"; one community per day, not 3 in one hour.
- **Paid sponsorship zero conversions** = bad sign for paid unit economics. Mitigation: only one $1200 test; pre-arranged exit if first placement converts ≤2.
- **Outlook IT departments block tldrof.com** = unlikely but possible. Mitigation: dedicated privacy/security page Sarah can forward to IT; explicit "we never touch your inbox" hero.
