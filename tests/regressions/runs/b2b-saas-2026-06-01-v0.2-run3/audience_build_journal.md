# Audience Build Journal — Threadline

Template for tracking the 90-day audience-build cadence. Fill weekly. Be honest. If a week underperforms, write WHY.

---

## Weekly cadence targets (per founder)

- 1 long-form essay published (Substack + cross-posted to HN if it fits)
- 5 substantive HN comments / day (on PM-tool / eng-team scaling threads)
- 1 podcast pitch sent
- 25 cold outreach messages sent (design-partner-candidate tech leads at named target companies)
- Daily presence in Rands Leadership Slack (helpful, not promotional)
- 1 design-partner sync (30 min) with each active design partner

## Failure-mode triggers (re-run /plan-cmo-review if any fire)

- Month 1 essays <50% of target → cadence not sustainable; revisit time allocation.
- Month 1 cold-outreach replies <2 → outreach quality or target list is wrong; revisit.
- Month 1 zero design partners → ICP definition or founder-network mismatch is structural; revisit.
- Month 2 Vohra <20% → product wedge is wrong; revisit positioning.
- Month 2 zero sponsored placement booked → either funding constraint or sponsor relationships unbuilt; revisit.
