# Launch Playbook — Threadline

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Source:** Section 5 of marketing_plan.md, expanded.

---

## Launch-gate decision

**The original gate ("5 paying companies by day 30") is rejected.** Mid-market procurement runs 90-180 days; the calendar gate is mathematically impossible via inbound and only superficially achievable via friends-of-friends (which the founders cannot actually fill — their network is FAANG-heavy, not Series B-heavy).

**Replacement gate (PMF-signal):**
- **Design-partner cohort fired:** 5-10 design partners actively using Threadline daily by day 60.
- **Vohra "very disappointed" ≥40%** in survey at end of pilot (day 75-90).
- **Word-of-mouth signal:** ≥2 design partners unprompted refer Threadline to a peer.
- **Founder readiness:** founder presence built sufficient enough in the audience that the launch will not echo into silence (at least 1k engaged Substack subscribers AND at least one Pragmatic Engineer or Lenny's sponsored placement queued).

If all three fire → public launch ~day 90.
If two of three fire → another 30-day iteration.
If one or zero → fundamental re-design of motion (likely the "Linear add-on" reframe from outside voice).

## Phase 0 — Pre-launch runway (Days -90 to -1)

### Days -90 to -60
- Each founder publishes 1 long-form Substack essay per week (8 total in month).
- Cold outreach: 50 design-partner-candidate tech leads contacted (hyper-personalized; ask for 30 min of perspective, NOT a sales pitch).
- Daily HN comment presence (15 min, 5 substantive comments/day, on PM-tool and eng-team scaling threads).
- 3 EM podcast pitches sent.
- 5 design partners onboarded (week 4 goal); founder runs weekly 30-min sync with each.
- Lurk + contribute in Rands Slack daily — genuine helpfulness, zero promotion.

### Days -60 to -30
- 1 sponsored issue in Pragmatic Engineer OR Lenny's Newsletter (timing: 30 days before launch to seed audience, NOT on launch day itself — launch day needs the spike + sponsorship reinforces a week later).
- Design-partner cohort grows to 8-10; Vohra survey administered at week 8.
- 1 podcast appearance recorded.
- Continue 1 essay / founder / week. Topics shift to "what we're learning from design partners" (case-study mining).
- First 1-2 design partners commit to be public reference customers (logos + quotes for launch).
- Cold outreach pace continues: 25 new targets / week.

### Days -30 to -1
- Final Vohra survey administered; gate decision made at day -14.
- Homepage refreshed with design-partner logos + quotes.
- Founders pre-write Show HN post (the most polished writing they will produce all year).
- Soft email pre-announcement to engaged Substack subscribers (~1-2k by now): "We're going public next week; here's the story."
- Twitter / X teaser thread queued.
- LinkedIn post queued (different audience, more director-eng-heavy).
- 2nd sponsored placement scheduled to run launch + 7 days.
- Design partners briefed: "We launch Tuesday at 9am Pacific. We'd love it if you replied/quote-tweeted with your honest experience."

## Phase 1 — Launch day + first 48 hours (T = 0)

### Pre-launch (T - 24h)
- Final sweep: status page up, support email monitored, Stripe checkout tested, sign-up flow tested in 3 browsers, demo account ready.
- Two founders agree on a 12-hour shift schedule for HN comment coverage.

### T = 0 (Tuesday 9:00am Pacific)
- **Show HN post goes live.** Title format: "Show HN: Threadline – Project tracking for engineering teams (better roadmap rollup than Linear)". Body leads with the wedge (cross-team roadmap rollup), names design-partner companies, includes 1-2 quotes, ends with a humble "what would you want to see — happy to answer questions."
- Founder A monitors HN comments full-time for the first 6 hours.
- Founder B writes ONE Twitter thread at T+15 min linking to the HN post.

### T + 2h
- If HN post is on rising / front page → Founder B tweets thread (boosted by design partners).
- If HN post is dying (<10 upvotes) → DO NOT panic-share or solicit upvotes (against HN rules). Pivot to long-form Substack post that does not require HN: "Why we built Threadline (and what we learned launching it)."

### T + 4h
- LinkedIn post from each founder. Topic: more director-eng-friendly framing — security, vendor stability, team output.
- Email blast to engaged Substack subscribers + personal network (~50 + 1-2k).

### T + 8h
- Each founder spends 4 hours in HN comments — substantive, humble, technical.
- Direct response to every top-level comment.
- No bouncing to bug reports until launch crunch ends.

### T + 24h
- Compliant cross-post in Rands Slack (only if community rules allow; the founders have been participating for 90 days at this point so the share is welcomed, not spammed).
- Compliant cross-post in IndieHackers (lower priority for B2B but free top-of-funnel).
- Founder daily-recap Substack post: "24 hours into our launch — here's what's happened."

### T + 48h
- Compliant Reddit posts: r/ExperiencedDevs, r/engineeringmanagers. Frame as "We built X — happy to answer questions" not "We built X — try it now."
- Email follow-up to anyone who started a trial in first 48h.

## Phase 3 — Launch month (Days 8-30)

- 1 podcast appearance / week (2-4 total).
- Continue 1 essay / founder / week (8 essays in month 1 post-launch).
- Daily founder presence in HN, Rands Slack, technical Twitter.
- Trial-to-paid follow-up: personal email from a founder to every trial that has 3+ active users.
- Cold outreach pace continues: 25 named accounts / week.
- Track every sign-up's UTM source; weekly dashboard review.

---

## Risk callouts

- **HN miss without design-partner logos = lottery loss.** Mitigated by audience-build runway AND design-partner cohort BEFORE Show HN.
- **All-on-Twitter:** mitigated by 5-channel sequence (HN, Twitter, LinkedIn, sponsored newsletter, podcast).
- **Single Reddit subreddit ban:** mitigated by participating genuinely in 3+ subs for 60+ days before posting.
- **Negative comment storm on HN:** prepare 3 humble, technical, design-partner-grounded responses to the predictable objections ("Linear already does this," "$40 is too expensive," "Who are you?"). Do not get defensive in public.
- **Founders burn out mid-launch week:** schedule explicit 12-hour shift rotations for HN comments. The launch week is a sprint; do not pretend it isn't.

---

## Success criteria for launch (90 days post-launch)

- 100-300 trial sign-ups (most will not convert; that's normal)
- 10-30 active trial teams (3+ users active in week 4)
- 5-10 design-partner upgrades to paid (these were already warm)
- 2-5 cold-trial paying customers (best case; could be zero if launch is sub-front-page)
- 1-2 inbound pipeline conversations from sponsorship reach
- 2k+ Substack subscribers (cumulative since pre-launch)

**What to do at day 90 if these are not hit:**
- Re-run `/plan-cmo-review` with launch retrospective data.
- Pivot consideration: the "Linear add-on" reframe from outside voice, or vertical wedge (ML/AI infra teams specifically).
- Founder skill-gap consideration: is the third co-founder (marketer/designer with audience) necessary?
