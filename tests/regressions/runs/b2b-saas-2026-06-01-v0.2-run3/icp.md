# ICP Specification — Threadline

**Date:** 2026-06-01
**Source:** Section 2 of marketing_plan.md, expanded.

---

## Primary persona — Priya, tech lead, Series B SaaS

### Identity
- 32 years old. Senior engineer / tech lead. Manages 4-6 engineers directly.
- Currently at a 120-person Series B fintech (think Mercury / Ramp / Brex tier).
- Career path: CS degree → 4 years at Stripe (or Datadog / Plaid / similar) → joined current company 2 years ago to lead a team.
- Reports to a director-eng who reports to a VP-eng. Has tool-selection input but not tool-purchase authority.

### Currently-paid adjacent products
- ChatGPT Plus ($20/mo personal)
- Substack subscriptions (~$50-100/yr total — Pragmatic Engineer paid, 1-2 others)
- Raycast Pro (~$8/mo personal)
- Company-paid: Linear, Slack, Notion (docs), GitHub, Datadog, Vercel/AWS, Figma (rare)

### Where she spends attention RIGHT NOW
1. **Pragmatic Engineer newsletter** (Gergely Orosz) — 800k+ subs, reads weekly. Highest-trust technical voice in her world.
2. **Lenny's Newsletter** (Lenny Rachitsky) — 825k+ subs, reads weekly. Strongest signal on PM-and-leadership topics.
3. **Rands Leadership Slack** (Michael Lopp) — active in 2-3 channels, lurks in 10+.
4. **LeadDev** — reads articles, attended 1 conference in past 2 years.
5. **HackerNews front page** — daily scroll, 20 min/day.
6. **Technical Twitter/X** — ~2k followers herself; follows ~300 accounts in tech.
7. **2-3 specific Substacks** — e.g., "Engineering Enablement," individual senior engineers she admires.
8. **Conference talks (on YouTube)** — LeadDev, StaffPlus, occasional QCon.

### What she complains about in adjacent categories
- **Linear:** "Cross-team roadmap rollup is bad." "Quarterly planning lives in Notion or Google Sheets, not Linear." "Reporting features are thin." "Plus tier is pricey for what you get."
- **Jira:** "Bloated." "Slow." "Custom workflows are a maintenance burden no one wants." "My ICs hate it."
- **Notion (as a quasi-PM tool):** "Not real PM." "Slow at scale." "Falls apart for sprint tracking."
- **Asana:** "Too business-y." "Not for engineers."

### Specific objections she would raise to Threadline
1. "We just migrated to Linear 6 months ago — switching costs are real."
2. "What does this do that Linear doesn't? 'Engineering-specific' is vague."
3. "Will my ICs actually use it, or will they keep opening Linear out of habit?"
4. "$40/seat is 5x what we pay for Linear. Why?"
5. "Is this a real company with funding and runway, or two guys who'll bail in a year?"
6. "Do you have SOC 2? SSO? Our security team will block it without those."

### Specific reasons she would convert
1. **Cross-team roadmap rollup that genuinely works** — Linear's biggest gap at her scale.
2. **Quarterly planning view** that doesn't require Notion + Google Sheets duct-tape.
3. **First-class sprint + epic + roadmap integration** without configuration hell.
4. **Founder-presence + design partner program** — feels like she'd be heard.
5. **A trusted voice in her audience recommended it** (Gergely, Lenny, a peer in Rands Slack).

## Secondary buyer — Director / VP of Engineering ("Marcus")

### Identity
- 42 years old. Director or VP of Engineering at the same 120-person company. Manages 4-6 tech leads (Priya is one of his direct reports).
- Career path: senior engineer → eng manager → senior EM → director → VP. 18 years total.
- Cares about: team output, hiring + retention, security/compliance posture, vendor risk, predictable burn.

### Where he spends attention
- Pragmatic Engineer (subscriber)
- Lenny's (occasional)
- Rands Slack (more active than Priya, in different channels)
- LeadDev articles
- VP/CTO peer Slack groups
- LinkedIn (more than IC engineers)

### What he cares about that Priya doesn't
- **Security: SOC 2 Type II, SAML/SSO, audit logs, data residency.**
- **Vendor stability:** "What's your funding? Will you exist in 3 years?"
- **Predictable cost:** "What does this scale to as we hit 250 engineers?"
- **Support SLA:** "Who do I call when it breaks during a release?"
- **Procurement collateral:** MSAs, DPAs, security questionnaires answered.

### Objections he raises (after Priya brings it to him)
1. "Security review will take 4-6 weeks. Do they have a SOC 2 report?"
2. "What's the 3-year cost model at our growth rate?"
3. "Is there a Linear migration path or are we re-entering all our issues?"
4. "Who else in our space uses this? Reference customers?"

### What converts him
- A founder who can show up to a 30-min call and answer the security + reference questions calmly.
- Design-partner logos from companies he respects (peer Series B's, ideally one company he's heard of).
- A clear "Linear migration tool" that imports issues/projects.

---

## Anti-persona (do NOT target)

- Indie hackers / solo founders — wrong scale, wrong willingness-to-pay, wrong product fit.
- Enterprise (1000+ engineer orgs) — they will out-procurement Threadline and demand features that contradict the wedge.
- Non-engineering teams (design, marketing, product) — these segments use Asana / Monday / Notion; Threadline's engineering specificity is anti-positioning for them.

---

## Validation plan

The Priya composite is a **hypothesis** until 10 real Priyas confirm it. Recruit 10 design-partner-candidate tech leads in the next 30 days. After 10 interviews:
- Confirm or revise the attention-surface list (which newsletters / communities / podcasts).
- Confirm or revise the objection list.
- Confirm or revise the convert-reasons list.
- Compare Priya across the 10 interviews: how much variance? If high variance, the persona may be 2 personas (e.g., "fintech Priya" vs "AI/ML Priya") that need different go-to-market.
