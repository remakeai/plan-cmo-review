## Trigger conditions for action

- **3 weeks with audience time <30%** → STOP and rebalance; product velocity isn't the bottleneck, distribution is
- **4 weeks without newsletter growth** → newsletter CTA position / lead magnet broken; A/B fix
- **8 weeks without referral-from-customer signal** → product isn't load-bearing for paying users; investigate retention/dependence
- **Day 60 without launch readiness** → push launch by 2 weeks; quality > calendar for dev tool launches (per Section 5a discussion)
