# AuthForge — Audience Build Journal (template)

Track weekly cadence. Fill in. Re-review during 30-day self-check.

## Weekly commitment baseline (~7 hours/week, ≥40% of pre-launch time)

| Activity | Target | Hours/week |
|---|---|---|
| Blog post writing | 2/month | ~2 |
| Twitter posts/threads | 3 posts + 1 thread / week | ~1.5 |
| Concierge outreach + interviews | 5–10 emails/week, 2–3 calls | ~2 |
| Podcast prep / pitching | rolling | ~1 |
| Newsletter | 1 email/week | ~0.5 |

## Milestones to hit

- **Week 4:** Newsletter at 200+ subs, 5 design partners enrolled, 1 podcast recording confirmed
- **Week 8:** Newsletter at 500+, 2+ podcasts live, 1 guest post placed, 3+ design partners using OSS daily
- **Week 12 (Day +60 launch):** Newsletter at 1,000+, gate-criteria met (3 of 5 DPs report dependence, 2 of 5 converted to paid)
