## Risk callouts for the Sprint

- **Burnout risk** (per outside voice): 13-16 hr/day for 2 weeks of Stage 1 + build + audience-build is dangerous. Reduce scope (defer picker fallback to v1.0.5) if exhaustion sets in by Day 10.
- **Lenny's Slack gatekeeping**: do NOT post promotional content before Day 30. Mute = channel destroyed.
- **DM-as-spam risk**: keep DMs personal, reference specific public content of the recipient. Template-DMs WILL get reported.
- **Substack inconsistency**: weekly Friday cadence MUST hold. One missed week early signals "this won't compound."
- **X follower-count obsession**: don't measure followers; measure **engagement per thread** (replies, quote-tweets, DMs received). Cf. skill Section 8.
