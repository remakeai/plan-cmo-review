## Cadence drift alarm

**Flag yourself if any of these are true:**

- [ ] Shipped <2 tutorials in day 1-30 window OR <1/wk in day 31-60 (red flag — Section 7 SEO compounding loop is at risk)
- [ ] Founder time on building exceeded 50% for 2 consecutive weeks (red flag — M7 ratio reverting)
- [ ] Discord office hours skipped for 2 consecutive weeks (red flag — community trust eroding)
- [ ] Named-adopter outreach paused (red flag — no logos = weak launch credibility)

**If 2+ flags raised:** founder schedules a recovery week. Cancel building work. Catch up on audience-build. Re-evaluate timeline.
