# Launch playbook — Threadline

**Gate:** PMF-signal-driven, not calendar. Launch fires when ≥3 design partners report ≥40% "very disappointed" AND 3+ pilots converted to paid annual contracts. Realistic timeline: day 90, not day 30.

**Anchor:** Linear playbook (Twitter-led waitlist + handpicked invites + concierge onboard) blended with Superhuman PMF gate. Explicitly NOT a Show HN-led launch.

## Risk callouts

1. **HN miss is now LOW impact** because LinkedIn is the lede surface and design-partner proof is the trust anchor. Pre-v0.3 plan was HN-as-only-channel, which is single-point-of-failure.
2. **LinkedIn algorithm shift** is real risk but recoverable via podcasts + newsletter cadence.
3. **Rands Slack ban** for self-promo is real if founders post without genuine prior presence. Mitigation: 90+ days of genuine participation before any launch-related post. If not yet built, skip Rands during launch.
4. **Design partner pulls out before launch** (e.g., gets acquired, key person leaves, contract renegotiates). Mitigation: have 5+ design partners signed; assume 30% attrition.
5. **Procurement still pending on launch day** for a pilot company → "in pilot" not "paying" → public messaging carefully distinguishes pilot count from paid count.

## What this playbook explicitly REJECTS

- Show HN as the sole channel
- Calendar gate ("launch by day 30 regardless of signal")
- Launch with no design partners and no social proof
- "Better Linear" positioning in launch copy
- Superlatives ("revolutionary," "best-in-class") in dev-facing copy
- Free trial without sales motion as the primary acquisition mechanic
