## Anti-patterns to refuse in this journal

- "I'll start next week" repeated 3+ weeks in a row → admit the priority isn't real and restructure
- "Posted on Twitter" without specific content → does not count
- "Was on a call" without named person → does not count as an interview
- Counting build-in-public tweets toward audience-build if they get <10 engagements → measure the signal, not the activity

## Re-review trigger

Re-run `/plan-cmo-review` at day 30 to evaluate progress against this journal. If outputs are <50% of targets, the priority isn't holding and a structural change (hire, scope cut, raise) is needed.
