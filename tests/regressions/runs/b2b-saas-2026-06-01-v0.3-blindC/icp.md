# ICP — Threadline

## Headline

**Director of Engineering at a 100-500 person Series B-D SaaS** (US-based, English-first), currently using Linear and/or Jira with cross-team aggregation pain, with budget authority for tooling decisions up to ~$50/seat without VP sign-off.

NOT: ICs (recommenders, not buyers). NOT: VP Eng at 1000+ org (different procurement). NOT: founders / CTOs at <50-person companies (they buy Linear free tier and never upgrade until they grow).

## Composite persona — "Priya"

| Attribute | Detail |
|---|---|
| Role | Director of Engineering |
| Company stage | Series C SaaS, ~220 engineers across 12 teams |
| Vertical | Fintech (representative; could be healthtech, dev-tools-SaaS, vertical-SaaS) |
| Location | NYC or SF, hybrid 3 days in office |
| Career arc | IC → tech lead at Stripe-class company → EM at Brex-class → Director at current |
| Direct reports | 4 EMs reporting to her, ~12 teams under those |
| Tools she controls | Linear (4 teams, modern stack), Jira (4 teams, legacy from acquisition), Notion (docs), Slack, GitHub, DataDog, PagerDuty, Sentry |
| Budget authority | Up to ~$50/seat without VP-Eng approval; above that requires sign-off |
| Procurement reality | New vendor adds 4-6 weeks: legal review (DPA), security review (SOC2 attestation), budget approval. Once vendor is approved, expansion within company is fast. |

## What she complains about

- "Quarterly planning takes 6-8 hours/week for me personally because I have to manually aggregate status across Linear and Jira"
- "Status reports to my VP are a manual rollup hell"
- "Migrating the Jira teams to Linear means losing 4 years of ticket history and breaking everyone's saved filters; not worth the org-disruption"
- "Linear is opinionated; some of my teams need configurability for their compliance workflows. Linear's response is 'we don't do that'"
- "I want one truth across all my teams without forcing tool migration"

## Specific objections she will raise to Threadline

1. **"Why would I pay $40/seat when Linear is $16?"** → unless wedge justifies the 2.5x premium, this kills the deal
2. **"Who else like me uses this?"** → cold-start trust problem; first design partner is the hardest
3. **"What's your SOC2 status?"** → if no SOC2 attestation, can't pilot at most 100+ person SaaS
4. **"How do I migrate from Jira without losing history?"** → migration story is load-bearing if pitched as Jira-replacement
5. **"What happens if you get acquired or shut down? My team is now stuck"** → vendor risk; needs answer
6. **"My team just spent 6 months adopting Linear. Now you want me to migrate again?"** → switching cost from Linear is HIGHER than from Jira because Linear-users are happy
7. **"Show me the security questionnaire response"** → standard mid-market gate

## Reasons she would convert

1. **Cross-tool aggregation** — if Threadline genuinely solves the "Linear + Jira in one view" pain without forcing either team to migrate, this is a unique offer none of the competitors deliver
2. **Quarterly planning automation** — if Threadline auto-generates the status rollup she manually builds today, she gets back 6 hours/week, which at her comp dwarfs $40/seat × team
3. **Named peer reference** — if 1-2 Directors she trusts (Rands acquaintance, ex-Stripe peer, LeadDev speaker she follows) report dependence, she'll pilot
4. **Procurement-friendly:** vendor has SOC2, standard DPA, founder responsive to security questionnaire within 48 hours

## Validation requirement before this ICP is treated as truth

Founders must conduct 5+ Priya-shaped user interviews by day 14. Specifically ask:
- "Walk me through your last quarterly planning week"
- "What's your most-expensive-per-seat tool that your team approves and uses daily?"
- "What did you reject for quality reasons that was cheaper than your accepted tool?"
- "If a peer Director recommended a new project tracking tool, what would make you take the meeting?"
- "What's your SOC2 / DPA / security questionnaire process for new tools?"

If 5 interviews fail to validate this composite, the ICP itself needs revision before launch.
