## Notes for future weeks

The hardest cadence to hit is cold outreach (5/week sustained) because it requires identifying real named humans in the paying-ICP profile. The founder will likely run out of network contacts by week 3-4 and need to expand via:
- LinkedIn 2nd-degree search ("In-House Counsel" + "fintech" + Chicago area filter)
- Sector publication LinkedIn (legal-tech voices, CFO community voices)
- Alumni network (university, ex-Micron)
- Mutual-connection requests via warm intros

When cold-outreach pipeline runs dry, the founder must commit to outreach-pipeline-building as its own work item, NOT skip the cadence.

## Failure modes (from prior plans)

- "I'll catch up next week" — almost never happens. Missed week compounds.
- "The cold-outreach response rate is too low, I'll batch them" — batching hides the cadence drop. Even 1/week sustained is better than 0/week with periodic batches.
- "I'll write longer posts less often" — algorithm rewards frequency. Better: 2 medium posts than 1 long post.
- "Founder energy goes to building during build calendar" — exactly why pre-launch audience-build mostly fails. The plan accepts this and front-loads what little audience-build the pre-launch window can hold.
