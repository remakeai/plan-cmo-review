## Specific objection patterns

- **Confidentiality / privilege / data-residency:** load-bearing. Privacy architecture (forwarding address, no OAuth, no inbox access) is the answer; landing page must lead with it for these personas.
- **IT approval friction:** "Will my IT department block this?" Answer: the forwarding architecture sidesteps IT entirely; founder-positioned trust signal.
- **Subscription fatigue / pricing skepticism:** "Why should I add another monthly?" Answer: annual billing as expensible single line item.
- **AI hallucination / accuracy:** "Will it summarize away what matters?" Answer: include source links to original; show the work; per-newsletter slider (eventually) lets user tune aggressiveness.

## Required pre-launch validation

Founder MUST conduct 5-10 user-research calls with named M365/corporate/regulated knowledge workers in the next 14 days before pivoting the entire positioning. Specific commitments:

- Identify 10 candidates from extended network (founder's college network, friends-of-friends, LinkedIn 2nd-degree)
- 30-min calls; questions:
  - "Walk me through how you read newsletters each morning."
  - "What have you tried? Why did it fail?"
  - "If I told you about a tool that delivered all your newsletters as one digest in 60 seconds, no OAuth needed, $15/mo — would you try it?"
  - "What would make you NOT try it?"
- Document responses; if <3 of 10 validate the pain at the intensity assumed, do NOT pivot ICP — ship to HN cohort with explicit v1.5 corporate-pivot planned.

This is non-optional. The pivot is currently a hypothesis; the calls turn it into evidence.
