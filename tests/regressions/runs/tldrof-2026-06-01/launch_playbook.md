# Launch Playbook — tldrof.com

Generated by `/plan-cmo-review` Section 5, on 2026-06-01

> **Gate:** Do NOT execute Day 0 until the `audience_build_sprint.md` 30-day cadence has run and hit its day-30 targets (100-300 ICP-relevant followers/subscribers, 2-3 newsletter-author relationships, ≥1 launch-week cross-mention secured, ≥10 build-in-public posts published). **A launch with zero warm audience is the "Hidden Signal: 2 upvotes" outcome.** ([reference](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=46595859))

## Non-negotiables (per skill)
- **≥3 channels.** Show HN alone is a stop-condition.
- **Sequenced, not simultaneous.** Each channel feeds the next.
- **Warm before cold.** The launch lands on a primed micro-audience built in the sprint.

## Launch-day operating notes

- **Every link gets a distinct UTM** (`?utm_source=hn` / `_reddit` / `_phunt` / `_<authorname>`). This is how you learn which channel actually produces *paying* users (the Meco lesson).
- **Watch activation, not upvotes.** The live metric on launch day is: signups → completed magic moment → trial starts. HN points are vanity (and emotionally hazardous).
- **Have the "$9 vs $4.90 Readless" answer pre-written** and paste it the moment the first commenter asks (they will).
- **Founder must resist operator-shape instinct:** when a user hits an onboarding snag in the HN comments, the fix is to improve the self-serve flow and reply with the fix, not to manually hand-onboard each person.

## Risk callouts (per skill)

- **HN miss = no recovery if it's the only channel.** Mitigated by the parallel newsletter-author cross-mentions (Day +10-14) and Product Hunt (Day +7) — the launch is *not* all-in on one HN post.
- **All-on-X = no recovery if the algorithm shifts.** Mitigated: X is an amplifier here, never the primary channel.
- **Single subreddit ban = no recovery.** Mitigated: two subs + IndieHackers + PH + author channel; read every sub's rules first; lead with value.
- **The launch firing before the sprint is done** is the dominant risk — the gate at the top of this doc exists to prevent exactly that.

---

## Fallback if the day-30 sprint targets are missed

Do **not** launch into silence to hit an arbitrary build-done date. Instead:
- Keep dogfooding (founder is paying customer #0).
- Extend the sprint 30 days, doubling down on whichever channel showed *any* signal.
- Pursue the **newsletter-first reframe** (run the free "TLDR of TLDRs" Substack and grow that list) until there's a warm audience worth launching to.
- The product is done; the audience is the missing input. Fix the input.
