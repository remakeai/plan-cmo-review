# Audience-Build Sprint — tldrof.com (30 / 60 / 90 day)

Generated by `/plan-cmo-review` Section 4, on 2026-06-01

> **Why this is the load-bearing artifact:** M3 = ~0 ICP-relevant audience, M7 = 0% of time on audience-building. The product is being built well; it is being built toward an empty room. This sprint fills the room *before* launch. **The launch (`launch_playbook.md`) is gated behind the day-30 targets here.**

## The core reframe (do this and the rest follows)

**Launch the newsletter before you launch the product.**

Start a **free public Substack: "The TLDR of TLDRs"** — you (manually/semi-manually) digest the top AI/eng newsletters 3×/week. This single move:
1. Builds the exact Priya-shaped audience *pre-product*.
2. *Is* a live, public demo of the product's value.
3. Creates relationships with the newsletter authors you summarize (your #1 distribution channel).
4. Gives the eventual Show HN a warm list to convert ("loved the free digest? here's the personalized, automated app").

You already have the engine (your own v1 pipeline, or just do it by hand for 30 days). Use it on yourself in public.

## Days 1-30 — Start a fire from zero

**Targets by day 30:** 100-300 ICP-relevant subscribers/followers · 2-3 newsletter-author relationships opened · ≥10 build-in-public posts live · ≥1 launch-week cross-mention verbally secured.

**Weekly cadence (every week, non-negotiable — this is the ~30% of time from M7):**

| Output | Count / week | Where | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Free "TLDR of TLDRs" Substack issue | **3** | Own Substack | The core asset. Each issue ends with the **real "minutes saved" math**. |
| Build-in-public post | **2** | 1 HN comment-thread presence + 1 substantive post on r/SideProject OR IndieHackers, rotating | Always carry a real screenshot (the time-saved card). Never pitch; show. |
| Newsletter-author outreach emails | **5** | Email | AlphaSignal, TLDR/TLDR AI, Lenny's, Pragmatic Engineer, Latent Space orbit, Bensbites, etc. Ask: "I digest your newsletter in my free roundup — want to see it? Open to a cross-mention?" |
| Reply/engage | daily | HN, the two subreddits, X replies to newsletter authors | Be a known, helpful, specific commenter on newsletter-overload / inbox topics. |

**Topics to cover (so you're never staring at a blank page):**
- "I read 8 AI newsletters this week so you don't have to" (recurring format = the Substack).
- The architecture of forwarding-not-OAuth (technical credibility; HN catnip).
- "How much time do newsletters actually cost you?" (the measured-time-saved thesis, with your own real numbers).
- Honest competitive teardowns: you using Readless / Meco / Digest, what's good, what's missing (this *is* your positioning, and it's link-bait the ICP searches for).
- Backlog-bankruptcy: "I had 73 unread newsletters. Here's what clearing them in one session looked like."

## Days 31-60 — Double down on what moved

- **Identify the one channel** from days 1-30 that produced the most ICP-relevant subscribers per hour of effort (your journal tells you). Pour ~60% of audience time there.
- **Convert author relationships into commitments:** turn the 2-3 warm authors into ≥1 confirmed launch-week cross-mention / guest-digest slot.
- **Line up 10-15 Product Hunt supporters** from the now-existing audience (DM your most-engaged followers).
- **Stand up the landing page** for `tldrof.com` with the A/B headline variants from the design doc (bankruptcy / bouncer / time-saved). Collect a pre-launch waitlist email from every Substack reader who wants the *automated personalized* version.
- **Target by day 60:** 300-600 ICP subscribers · waitlist of 100+ · ≥1 confirmed launch-week author cross-mention · PH supporters lined up.

---

## Days 61-90 — Integrate the launch

- Execute `launch_playbook.md` on top of the warm audience and waitlist.
- The free Substack readers + waitlist are the first conversion pool (warm, ICP-pure).
- Newsletter-author cross-mentions fire in launch week (the proven channel).
- Day-30 post-launch retro (real numbers) feeds the *next* cohort and seeds SEO.

## What this sprint explicitly refuses

- **"I'll post more on Twitter."** Vague, no audience to post to, demoted. Build-in-public *produces* the X presence as a side effect; don't depend on it.
- **Any time spent marketing to the 996 LinkedIn semiconductor followers.** Wrong audience for both ICPs. Zero launch energy there.
- **Open-ended "build audience eventually."** The cadence above is measurable per week; track it in `audience_build_journal.md`.

## The honest framing for the founder

You have a 16-18 day build and a great product coming. The trap is finishing the build and immediately Show-HN-ing into an empty room. **Decouple the launch date from the build-done date.** Finish building, keep dogfooding, and run this sprint until the room is full. A great product shipped to nobody still misses the 15-paying-by-day-14 gate. Audience is the input you're currently spending 0% on; this sprint is how you fix that.
