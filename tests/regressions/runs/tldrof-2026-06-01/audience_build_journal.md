## Launch gate (copy from sprint)

Do not execute Day 0 of `launch_playbook.md` until:
- [ ] 100-300 ICP-relevant subscribers/followers
- [ ] 2-3 newsletter-author relationships opened
- [ ] ≥1 launch-week cross-mention verbally secured
- [ ] ≥10 build-in-public posts live
- [ ] Landing page live with A/B headline variants + waitlist capture

## Self-check

Re-run `/plan-cmo-review` at day 30 against these targets. If unmet, extend the sprint — do not launch into silence.
