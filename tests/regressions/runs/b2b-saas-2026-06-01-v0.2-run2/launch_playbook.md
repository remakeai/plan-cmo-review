# Threadline — Launch Playbook

**Status:** CONTINGENCY plan. Primary recommendation is Mode C (Audience-Build Sprint) — do NOT launch in 30 days. This playbook applies only if PMF gate is met (≥40% "very disappointed" + ≥6 design partners hitting engagement gate + SOC 2 Type 1 in flight + Pragmatic Engineer / Lenny placement scheduled).

## Launch gate

**Replaces founder's "5 paying companies by day 30" calendar gate.**

PMF gate (Superhuman precedent):
- ≥40% of design-partner cohort responds "very disappointed" if Threadline went away — measured via in-product survey at 30+ days of use
- ≥60% of design-partner team's eng staff use Threadline ≥4 days/week for ≥4 consecutive weeks
- ≥2 unprompted referrals from design partners ("can I introduce you to X?")

If gate not met by day 90: iterate with design-partner cohort. Do not launch.

## Risk callouts (per skill v0.2)

- **HN miss = no recovery if it's the only channel.** Mitigation: 5+ channels in the first 72 hours (LinkedIn, Twitter, email list, Pragmatic Engineer slot, Reddit AMA, IH cross-post).
- **All-on-Twitter = no recovery if algorithm shifts.** Mitigation: LinkedIn + Email are the durable channels; Twitter is supplementary.
- **Single Reddit subreddit = no recovery if banned.** Mitigation: AMA only, mod-approved, NO promotional posts in other subs.
- **Show HN flop pattern: <30 upvotes in 2 hours = no front page.** Mitigation: do NOT spam upvote (HN bans for this). If 2-hour mark passes without traction, pivot effort entirely to LinkedIn + email + DMs. Don't burn the day chasing HN recovery.
- **Press blackout (no TechCrunch / Information pickup).** Mitigation: don't depend on press; treat it as bonus, not plan.
- **Procurement-cycle reality:** even with a perfect launch, expect ZERO paying companies in week 1, 1-3 in month 1 (warm/design partners), 5-15 in month 3, 15-50 in month 6.
