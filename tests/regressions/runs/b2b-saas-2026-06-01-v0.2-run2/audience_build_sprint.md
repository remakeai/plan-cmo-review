# Threadline — Audience-Build Sprint (Mode C)

**Status:** PRIMARY artifact. Starts TODAY (2026-06-01). 90 days. Goal: build the prerequisite audience + design-partner cohort + pricing/positioning clarity so that a real launch becomes possible at T+90 (= 2026-08-30) or T+120.

## Why this sprint exists

Founders have ~0 ICP-relevant audience, zero B2B sales experience, calendar gate misaligned with mid-market cycle, pricing 2.5-4x competitor band, and dogfood-to-buyer audience gap. Launching in 30 days against this baseline = launching to silence + missing validation gate + burning the founders' belief in their own ability to ship.

This sprint is the GO condition for a real launch.

## Design-partner outreach template

Subject line (LinkedIn DM or cold email):

> Quick ask — design partner for Linear-alt built for eng managers

Body:

> Hi Priya,
>
> Two of us (ex-Google eng) are building Threadline — a project tracker built around the **engineering manager's workflow** (cross-squad dependency tracking, AI standup/retro automation, the sprint-reporting-up-to-VP layer that Linear refuses to build).
>
> Recruiting 10 design partners. The offer:
> - Free 60-day pilot
> - 50% off year 1 if you continue
> - Weekly 30-min feedback call with me directly
> - Real influence on the roadmap
> - Case study rights are mutual (you can review/approve)
>
> Looking for eng managers at 50-500 person SaaS. Spotted your profile + your work at [SPECIFIC THING] — felt like a fit.
>
> Worth a 20-minute intro call?
>
> [Founder]
> [LinkedIn link]

**Critical: NOT a product pitch. Design-partner ASK.** Different conversion psychology. Pitches ask for purchase decision; design-partner asks position the founder as the supplicant ("I need your expertise to build this right") which is true and converts better.

**Tracking spreadsheet columns:** Name | Company | Title | LinkedIn URL | DM sent date | Response (Y/N/maybe) | Intro call date | Signed (Y/N) | Onboarded date | Engagement (days/week) | PMF survey response | Notes.

## Rules of engagement

1. **No product promo in Rands Slack for 60 days minimum.** Trust precedes mention. If a Rands member asks for a tool recommendation in #tools, you may then reply briefly + transparently.
2. **No fake reviews or fake design-partner quotes.** All testimonials must be real, signed-off by the partner.
3. **No vanity metrics.** Don't celebrate follower counts, HN upvotes, or signups. Celebrate: paying design partners + engagement-gate hits + PMF survey results.
4. **Document everything.** Future founders running this sprint should be able to read what worked, what didn't, and why.

## Stop conditions

- If at day 30 you have <3 design partners signed: outreach script is broken or ICP is wrong. Stop, debug, restart.
- If at day 60 you have <40% engagement on signed partners: product is not solving real pain. Stop, iterate product (don't launch).
- If at day 90 PMF gate not met: extend sprint another 30 days OR re-run `/plan-ceo-review` to question the entire wedge.
