# Threadline — ICP Specification

## Composite persona: Priya

**Role:** Engineering Manager
**Company:** Series B SaaS, ~150 employees, ~40 engineers across 4 squads
**Reports to:** VP Engineering
**Manages:** 12 engineers across 2 squads (one mature, one new)
**Tenure as manager:** 2 years (was an IC for 6 years before promotion)
**Discretionary budget:** ~$5K/year for team tooling without approval (= ~10 seats at $40/mo); above that requires VP Eng sign-off; above $25K requires CFO + procurement + SOC2

## Buyer-vs-user gap

| Layer | Person | Role in purchase |
|---|---|---|
| User | 12 ICs in Priya's squads | Daily users; will abandon if UX is bad; have NO purchase authority |
| Champion | Priya | The buyer for ≤$5K spend; the influencer above that |
| Approver | VP Eng | Approves $5K-$25K; requires Priya's case |
| Procurement gate | Finance + Security | SOC 2, vendor risk, redlines; adds 2-4 weeks |

**Implication:** acquisition must produce CHAMPIONS (Priyas), not just users. Bottom-up motion (HN, free tier IC adoption) produces users but not champions. The plan must give Priya a reason to champion — that's the marketing job.

## What she currently pays for in adjacent categories

- Linear: $10-16/seat (her team is on it)
- GitHub Copilot Business: $19/seat (rolled out to team)
- Slack: $7.25/seat (whole co)
- Notion: $10/seat (team docs)
- Figma: $15/seat (designers, some engs)
- Personal subs: ChatGPT Plus $20, Cursor $20, Pragmatic Engineer paid $20/mo

Pattern: comfortable at $10-20/seat for team tools; her own personal subs cap around $20/mo each. **$40/seat is OUTSIDE her reflex band.** Justification must be explicit and load-bearing.

## What she complains about in adjacent categories

- Linear: "great IC tool, but cross-team dependency tracking is weak; sprint reporting up to my VP is manual"
- Jira: "configuration hell, slow, ICs hate it"
- Notion: "fine for docs but not for tracking; slow at scale"
- Sprint planning: "I cobble together Linear + Notion + a Google Sheet + Slack threads + a meeting"
- 1:1s and roadmap: "I track in a personal Notion doc — there's no good tool for the manager-layer of eng work"
- Standup and retro automation: "AI summaries would save me 30 min/day if they were actually good"

**Pain insight:** the actual gap is the *manager layer of work* — sprint reporting up, dependency tracking across squads, 1:1 + career-progression tracking, AI-assisted standup/retro. The plan's "Linear-but-eng-specific" framing misses this. The manager-layer-of-work framing is the wedge.

## Objections she would raise to Threadline

1. "Why migrate off Linear? Switching cost is brutal."
2. "Who else is using this? I can't be the case study."
3. "Is it SOC 2? My security team requires it."
4. "$40/seat for what Linear does at $16? Show me the math."
5. "What happens to my data if you go under? Are you VC-backed? Profitable?"
6. "I tried Height/ClickUp/Asana — what makes you different?"
7. "Does it work with our existing GitHub + Slack + Notion + Linear stack?"

## Reasons she would convert

1. Her VP Eng (or a peer Priya respects) recommended it
2. It solves the manager-layer-of-work gap that Linear refuses to address
3. Generous design-partner program: free year, founder-led onboarding, real influence on roadmap
4. AI manager copilot that saves 30+ min/day
5. Per-mgr pricing (free ICs) — turns the budget conversation
6. Specific compliance/vertical fit (SOC 2 + SOC 2 for customers, audit trails)
