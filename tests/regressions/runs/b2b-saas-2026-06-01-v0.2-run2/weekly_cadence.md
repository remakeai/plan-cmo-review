# Threadline — Weekly Cadence (Mode C Sprint)

**Repeats every week for 13 weeks (June 1 - Aug 30, 2026).**

## Per-week commitments — each founder

### Audience-build (≥50% of work time during sprint)

| Day | Activity | Output | Time |
|---|---|---|---|
| Mon | Plan week's content topics + outreach batch | 1 doc | 1 hr |
| Mon | LinkedIn long-form post (founder 1) | 1 post (800-1500 words) | 2 hr |
| Mon | Twitter thread (founder 1) | 1 thread (8-15 tweets) | 1 hr |
| Tue | LinkedIn long-form post (founder 2) | 1 post | 2 hr |
| Tue | Twitter thread (founder 2) | 1 thread | 1 hr |
| Tue-Wed | Send 25-50 design-partner cold DMs | Tracking sheet updated | 2 hr |
| Wed | Design-partner intro calls (3-5/week) | Notes + signed agreements | 3 hr |
| Thu | Active design-partner check-ins (weekly 15-30 min each) | Feedback log | 3-5 hr |
| Thu | Rands Slack: ≥1 substantive thread contribution (NOT promo) | Slack post | 0.5 hr |
| Fri | Newsletter draft + send (Beehiiv) | 1 newsletter | 2 hr |
| Fri | Podcast pitch (2-3 sends to eng-mgmt podcasts) | Email drafts | 1 hr |
| Sat-Sun | Reply to LinkedIn/Twitter comments, engage with PE/Lenny content, lurk Rands | Engagement | 1-2 hr |

**Total audience-build per founder per week:** ~20-25 hours.

### Product/engineering (≤50% of work time during sprint)

Continue product build with **design partners shaping the roadmap.** Weekly feedback log from Thursday check-ins translates directly into next sprint's product priorities. NO new features without design-partner request.

## Weekly retrospective (Sunday, 30 min)

Both founders together:
- What posts/threads hit? (>1K views = hit; >10K = breakout)
- How many design-partner DMs sent? How many responses? How many calls? How many signed?
- What did design partners say in this week's check-ins?
- What product changes do we need to make based on feedback?
- What didn't work? Why? Adjust next week's plan.

Output: 1-pager update in `journal/week-NN.md`.

## Failure modes to watch

- **Founder burnout from sustained 20-hr/wk content + outreach.** If burnout hits at week 4-6, that's a signal to delegate (hire a content writer or VA for outreach). Don't slow down — change the resource model.
- **"This is too slow."** It IS slow. That's the point. Linear took 3 years. Notion took 5. Slack had a prior exit. There's no fast path for marketing-naive founders into mid-market B2B.
- **Design-partner "yes" → onboarded silence.** If a partner signs but doesn't engage within 2 weeks, the product isn't solving their pain. Diagnose immediately; don't paper over with metrics.
- **Pragmatic Engineer / Lenny ghost.** Most cold outreach to high-tier creators fails. Plan B: pay for sponsored slot ($5-15K) if relationship-building doesn't yield by day 60.
